# ⚖️ Legal Analytics | Executive & Management Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

Legal Analytics is a Business Intelligence solution developed in Power BI to transform legal data into strategic insights for decision-making.

The project was designed to address two distinct needs:

* Transparency for law firm clients
* Operational control for managers and legal teams

To achieve this, two complementary dashboards were developed, each focused on a specific audience.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Law firms often face two major challenges:

### Clients

Clients may struggle to understand the current status of their legal cases, the financial exposure involved, and whether their cases are receiving proper attention.

### Management

Legal managers need visibility into team performance, workload distribution, SLA compliance, and operational bottlenecks that may impact results.

---

## 💡 Solution

A two-layer analytical solution was developed:

### Executive Dashboard

Designed for law firm clients.

Provides visibility into:

* Active cases
* Closed cases
* Case status
* Legal stage
* Financial exposure
* Legal fees
* Days without activity
* Case timeline

**Goal:** Improve transparency and provide a clear overview of ongoing legal matters.

---

### Management Dashboard

Designed for legal managers and operational teams.

Provides visibility into:

* Cases per lawyer
* Case status distribution
* Case stage distribution
* SLA compliance
* Days without activity
* Case closure trends
* Most critical cases

**Goal:** Support operational decision-making and identify workflow bottlenecks.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Executive Dashboard

* Active Cases
* Closed Cases
* Provisioned Amount
* Estimated Liability
* Amount Paid
* Legal Fees
* Days Without Activity

### Management Dashboard

* Cases per Lawyer
* Cases Within SLA
* Cases Outside SLA
* Cases by Status
* Cases by Legal Stage
* Top Inactive Cases

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* Power BI

* Data Storytelling

---

## 📈 Business Insights

The solution enables users to:

* Monitor SLA compliance
* Identify operational bottlenecks
* Analyze workload distribution among lawyers
* Track financial exposure across the portfolio
* Increase transparency for clients
* Support data-driven decision-making

---

## 🚀 Results

The project delivers a dual-perspective view of legal operations:

* An executive layer focused on transparency and client communication.
* A management layer focused on operational efficiency and performance monitoring.

This solution demonstrates how Business Intelligence can be applied to legal operations by connecting business objectives, operational metrics, and strategic decision-making.
